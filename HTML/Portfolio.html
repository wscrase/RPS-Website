Our Completed Projects

Explore some of our recent work showcasing our expertise and commitment to quality.

Ashling Park Vineyard

We were proud to deliver this full-scale vineyard project near Chichester, taking it from an untouched greenfield site to a fully functioning destination. Our team handled every stage, including the construction of a winery complete with a restaurant and bar, a series of holiday lodges, and the installation of a new driveway and entrance gates. This project is a great example of our ability to combine quality construction with rural and hospitality-focused development.

Peartree

This stunning property in Wisborough Green underwent a full transformation, starting with a new driveway and complete renovation of the main house. We added an oak-framed garden room extension, fully landscaped the surrounding gardens, and built a new swimming pool with a bespoke oak pool house. Beneath the pool house, we also created a wine cellar, blending practicality with a touch of luxury. A great example of how we combine traditional materials with modern living.

Newgrove

This remarkable project involved the complete renovation of a rare Grade II* listed, 12-bedroom property in Petworth. Working under strict heritage guidelines, our team carried out extensive works including a full roof replacement, complete replumbing, and meticulous stone masonry to restore the exterior. We also carefully renovated the interiors, preserving the property’s historic charm while updating it to modern living standards. Projects of this nature demand exceptional attention to detail and craftsmanship, and we’re proud to have brought this historic home back to life while preserving its original character.

Equestrian Centre

We built this brand-new equestrian centre in the Sussex countryside, delivering everything from the ground up. The project included a stable block, a new driveway, and a 60x20m indoor sandschool, all designed to provide a practical and high-quality space for year-round use. It’s a great example of our experience in equestrian construction, where attention to detail and durability are key.

Farmhouse

Set in the heart of the Sussex countryside, this project involved the complete construction of a new farmhouse from the ground up. Designed to blend seamlessly with its rural surroundings, the build combined traditional character with modern finishes throughout. It’s a great example of our ability to deliver high-quality, bespoke homes in sensitive, countryside locations.
