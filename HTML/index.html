About Us

Founded in 2003, RPS Contractors is a trusted, family-run construction company with over two decades of experience delivering high-quality projects across Sussex, Surrey, and Kent. We specialise in groundworks, property renovations, new builds, and bespoke agricultural and equestrian construction. As a well-established team, we're proud to offer the capabilities of a larger contractor while maintaining the personal service and reliability of a family business. Our skilled workforce allows us to manage projects of all sizes efficiently and professionally, from domestic extensions to large-scale rural developments.
